Well, October is over. Yesterday was Halloween. talk about some good times to be
had. Didn't have many trick or treaters. So we didn't give out any candy. Zip,
zero, zilch. That's right, no one came to our door so we didn't give out any
candy. Oh well, maybe next year? Yeah, that's what I'm thinking. More candy for
us to eat...even though we're not *meant* to be eating candy. Such a terrible
situation to be in.

Oh well, I'll still eat the candy. I like the sour candy myself. Skittles has a
good sour candy out there. I've found that sour candy also helps me when I'm 
having an anxiety attack. I had a therapist tell me about that little secret. It's
quite a good thing to have in my toolbox. It gives a shock to the system in one
form or another. It's a good thing. Thank goodness that therapist told me about
sour candy.

I don't know where I would be without those who love me in this life. There are
so many things that can happen in life. So many terrible things that we don't want
to happen, yet they can happen anyway. There's not much anything I can do about
it all. I have to keep working forward, there's nothing else I can do about it.

Life comes at us without warning, it's a complex situation at times, and we really
do not have the opportunity to do what we want with it. That, my dear friends,
is what this life is all about.
