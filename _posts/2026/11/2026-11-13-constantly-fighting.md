Fighting to stay alive isn't an easy task for people. Some would have you
believe it's just breathing and living. It's much more than that. It's surviving
until your dying day. Both mentally and physically. When the mentality comes
into your life you have decisions to make. Not everything can make perfect sense
of course, that's not what this life is about. No, it's something more
complex...something unique and yet something betrayed. I don't know how to
explain it.

My my my.

Wouldn't it be interesting to grasp what this life is about without falling
surrender to those things that come to the mind that make you fall rain across
your face. Yes, life would be better if I could figure that out. If life had a
way about it, I would want to understand what's going on. It's not an easy
thought process of course. No, everything will eventually go away, and we'll
always be lost. Lost in a world that doesn't want us around. So why do we stay?
What's the purpose of it all? I don't understand it. I simply cannot grasp for a
single moment what this life would be like without struggle.

That's saying something about how difficult life is for me at times.

So I try to live hoping for a better day ahead of me that's not the same as
yesterday.
