Sometimes you have to accept things you cannot change. Maybe these things simply
don't want to change. Whatever the case this life will be different for
everyone. What's the phrase? Such is life? Yeah, it's something like that. There
are a lot of things that end up like that. Wanting doesn't always make sense.

Death is something to hope for, to wish for. What's there to fear about dying?
Give it up to God, let Him decide. I die a little more inside every day. It's
not my idea of a good time mind you, I wish I knew how all of this worked out.
But I don't have a clue. I do kow that two people who I love are dead. Mom, Dad,
I miss you so much.

So, who do I tell about this? Maybe no one. There are so many thoughts in my
mind. I can't separate all the thoughts that are coming from my head. Too many
damn thoughts going through there. It's a curse, that's what it is.

Death will come for all of us someday. There can't be another way to put it. A
sad state of affairs this life. No I don't think it works out in our favor.

What does death feel like? Is it the same for everybody? What's it like to
experience eath? I guess I will have to wait and see when that time comes for
me. In a way I want it to hurry. IN a way I want it to take its time. I suppose
death will come when it comes. The devil's in the details. But what of those
details?

Why this obsession with death all of a sudden? Doesn't quite make sense to me.
It isn't an ideal thing to obsess over mind you. Yet color me interested. I want
to know all the things that happen when you die. I'm sure it's not a pleasant
experience that much I can tell.

Why do we have to experience death to begin with? I'm not sure if I fully
understand it's true purpose. Maybe I won't understand it until that day comes
for me. Just make sure I'm not dancing with the devil.

They say not to wish for something unless you really want it. All I want is to
see my mom and dad again. They weren't supposed to die. There was so much they
ahd to offer the world. Maybe I'm just being selfish here. I don't really know.

Am I messed up in the head for wanting to cross over to the other side of life?
I can only express it as more than morbid curiosity. I don't know how to express
myself in this situation I find myself in.

Everyone is curious of something, aren't they? Well this is my curiosity okay?
Don't judge me by my curiosities or things I find fascinating.

It could be a phase for all I know. So many things in this life, and I am unable
to calm down long enough to make things have sense again. You know?

I don't know what to believe in anymore. If I could understand the basics of
life, I believe it would be easier to understand. But here I sit simply
wondering how this life will end, or if I can end it myself. Is that too dark of
a thing to ponder?

I believe I will embrace death when it comes for me. We can't have it all you
know, there's no way of that ever happening. I fear it's quite the opposite at
times.

Not much in this life will ever make sense. We have to take the good with the
bad at times I fear. Talk about a miserable way to exist. But if that's how it's
meant to be, then that's what's written. I cannot dictate the future just like
iA can't change the past.

Death will find me. That is all there is to it. Such a life and what do I have
to show for it? Oh, sure an accomplishment here and there, but if that's all
there is to it, what exactly is the point of living anymore? I don't know.

Let all of these thoughts go, there doesn't seem to be a good use for them. Not
now, not ever. Where the dead go, I cannot be joining them. Their destiny is for
them alone to control. Just like my destiny is for me to find out how any of its
meant to unfold or unravel. Talk about an annoying thought process, yet here I
am.

So much for a peaceful thought process, and yet I'm full of complex emotions. I
don't know how better to express myself. Feels like there's a lot up in the air
at the moment. Whatever happens will happen. I wish for simplicity’s sake, not
everything will be miserable. Sad days will come that's for sure. But they won't
always be there hovering like a shark.

Not everything in this life will make complete sense. It's a curse I must live
with. That's all there is to it. So, I must simply allow it to happen. Wow!
there is a lot to unpack, with all of that. I'm not sure where to begin with it
all.

I don't suppose it matters as it is. Did it ever matter? I'm not sure if I have
an answer to that self-imposed question. Maybe I will never know.

So, where does that leave me? I'm not sure if I understand the question and I
wrote the damn thing!

People don't seem to understand mental health issues. It's either by choice, or
they are ignorant and clueless to it all. It irritates me to no end. Even my own
family doesn't get it and they're family for crying outloud!

The same with being called *crazy* at times. I don't understand the need to call
people names like that. It makes no sense to me. I'm *not* crazy! Call me
anything else but crazy! Thank you.

If death comes knowing I'm going to tell them I want to drive into the
afterlife.

What is truth anymore? Do you understand any of it? I'm not sure if I do, it's a
confusing topic for me. No, thanks!

If life had a b sides[^bsides] to it, I wonder what that would be like. I
imagine it would be full of some amazing stories. You know the ones you can't
say in public? Yeah, those. Oh, stories that aren't appropriate for anyone.
Allow it to simply become. I don't know what that means. Let it simply go.
There's no reason for any of it. So forget about it all.

I don't try to glorify death. That's not my intentional. Not at all. I need to
focus on the good parts of living. But what exactly is that? I don't know. Such
a life was never meant to be lived, yet that's exactly what I did. It's not an
easy thing to discuss or describe. Life has been a difficult one. I feel at
times I don't belong to the human race. How to explain it. I don't fit in
anywhere. It's a sad fucking truth that I must live with on a daily basis. How
disappointing.

Keep me alive even if that's not what I want. Don't pull the plug, I'm not ready
to go quite yet. Save me a spot on the other side. I'll see you when I get
there.

Is that all it takes to stay alive? I'm not quite sure that's how any of it
tends to work out. Life is a confusing monster. I don't think I will ever
understand it all as a whole.

What is it about, this life that I don't understand? Everything. Well if that's
not a blanket statement, I don't know what is! I wish I could grab hold of some
understanding in this life, but I don't think I can do that! Not yet at least.

If I can let it all go, where does that leave me in this present moment? If it's
not in a good place, I don't think I will ever be able to get to a good place in
life. Isn't that what everyone wants? A good standing in life? Being remembered
for the good they did? Yeah, that.
