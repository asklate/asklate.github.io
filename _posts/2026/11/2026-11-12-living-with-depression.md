I have depression, more than just depression Major Depressive Disorder with
Psychotic Features. It sucks. Let's just call it what it is, a fucking disaster.
But it's life and if that's what life has given me, then that's what life has
given me and I have to be able to accept whatever happens and move on from there.
I don't know *why* life has chosen to give me this mental illness and everything
else that goes with it...the anxiety sucks. But I can deal with it. I *have* to
deal with it. There's nothing else I can do about it.

I'm sure God had some reason for giving me this challenge in life. If there is
a God that is. I'd like to think someone is in control over this life, but I can't
say for certain there is or isn't someone out there. There are so many viewpoints
when it comes to a supreme being out there in the cosmos that no one can agree
on any of it, I find that interesting. But I'm losing scope of the topic here.

Having depression can be crippling at times. There isn't much that goes on about
it that I can focus on all at once. So many moving parts it feels like. I'm not
sure if I'm even making sense right now. But that's how life tends to work for me,
it doesn't always make sense. Such a terrible thing in this universe and yet
I know it's there. Man I wish I could explain it better, but I can't. Feels like
I'm a million miles away at times. There doesn't seem to be a simple way to
overcome this thought process...everything feels like it's closing in on me and
I don't have a way out of it all.
