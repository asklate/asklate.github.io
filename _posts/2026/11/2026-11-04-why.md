I'd like to be able to think life has a purpose for all of us. I'm not sure how
that's meant to be of course, but if it doesn't have a purpose than what are we
doing here and what for? Why are we here to begin with? I don't know, I don't
understand what's going on in this life. It's all a shame, that's what it is.
A shame that nothing will become good from it all. We can't say for certain if
life will become something reasonable or worthwhile. That's all there is to it.
Life will be what it will be and if it isn't? Then that's simply how it will end
up. I can't say for certain that it's meant to be a good thing or a bad thing.
I can't say that at all. I can say that it is a unique mystery.

Mysteries are an interesting thought process in life. I'd like to think they
aren't that important at times, but they are. They can destroy us into a million
pieces given the right procedures. But, well, who's counting those to begin
with? I sure am not.

If I could attempt to say what is and isn't right in the universe today, I
wouldn't even try. There isn't much to figure out. This life will continue on,
and we won't be able to do anything about it. That's life.
