I'd like to post a question.

> Why This Life?

Seems like a simple question right? Yeah, that's what I was thinking. But it's
not an easy thought process. Everyone has questions in life, they don't try and
create answers, well most people don't try to create answers that aren't there
to begin with. At least I hope they don't. Answers will come, it just takes time.
At least that's what I've always been told.

On my desk is a worry stone. Or rock. It's shiny and I hold it once in a while.
It helps calm me down at times I feel. I'd like to be able to know what this life
is all about. I know there are people who *think* they know what this life is
about, but they don't really know. Do they? I highly doubt it. There's too much
drama in life at times. I wish I could grasp something in this life and have it
make sense. Nothing makes sense to me though. It's almost impossible to do anything
about it. That's what this life feels like at times. It's so damn confusing.

So, what am I going to do about it all? I don't know. That's the typical response
I get from it all, but then there are so many more things in life that don't feel
like they can become a true real answer to my questions I have.

Some people would tell you that religion is the key answer to all questions relating
to life. I doubt it. I'm not anti-religion or anti-God or anything like that, but
I don't believe in organized, man-made religions. Including the one I was brought
up in. So there's that.
