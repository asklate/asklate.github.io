There are times when life turns to fear. We don't always understand where the
fear comes from.

Everything and everyone dies. There doesn't have to be fear everywhere, but it
is at times. Death has no fear of anything. It comes nad goes, and we don't have
a clue how it's meant to be. So, everyone dies.

If I could avoid fear I would. What's to say fear follows us wherever we go?
It's quite a complicated topic. If life had a way about it, I think it would be
complex.

Nightmares will keep you up, fear will keep you alert. No one can say anything
about it. Talk about an annoying fact. Let it go. There's nothing to consider.

Nobody should die alone. It's a depressing thought. Depressing and not funny at
all. Talk about a royal fuck up.

Depression is quite a bitch at times. I don't know how else to put it. That's
all there is to it all. Being mentally exhausted is also a bitch. Nothing
manages to make sense. I wish I knew how to deal with it. If I could believe in
something it would be a miracle. I don't know how that is. Did I mention it's
all a mess right now?

Fine, I told someone I'm depressed. Apparently it wasn't a surprise to them.
Well there goes that and anything else which can or will happen. That's life for
you. don't get in the way of overthinking.

The problem with life is this. I don't want to be part of it anymore. Probably
wrong of me to say. But I don't give a damn about that. It's life you know? Life
is full of nonsense and that's all there is to it.

Some words don't need to be written down. They are known. Little bits of phrases
here and there. All for the betterment of no one. Whoever said them probably had
good intentions, but now years later those intentions are lost.

Sometimes in life all we want is to live and survive. Other times we want to
cease to exist. Why? Existing is difficult as it is. It's not an easy thing to
do. Sometime everything will come to an end. That's the way of life. No one can
change the ending. An ending is always coming. You can't stop it.
