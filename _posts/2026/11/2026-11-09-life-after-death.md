Ever wonder what this life would be like after death? Yeah, I wonder that at
times. There doesn't appear to be any actual answer on the matter at hand now
does there? No, I'm afraid not. It would be nice to be able to understand what
this life is all about after we die. But I highly doubt that will guarantee any
kind of answer to anyone. Life will be what it will be and that's all there is
to it.

If we wanted all the answers we have to die first to get them. Not sure if I'm
willing to die to get a bunch of answers that we don't know if they'll actually
be fruitful or not. Yeah, that's the way of the world it would seem. Life tends
to get in the way, and we have nothing to do about it. If Life had a way to get
through it all, I would want to know that secret. But I'm not sure if I'm ready
for such a thing to be made known to me.

Being in ignorance at times, that's where it's at. We aren't expected to know
everything or anything. That's life for you. Everytime life comes and goes we
don't have an answer to it all...then we are just spinning our wheels at
whatever might have been. That's what life feels like at times. I don't know if
I have all the answers in the world regarding that though. It's a thought
process to be sure, but still a thought process nevertheless. Talk about a
mindfuck.

I wish I could understand everything there is to understand about life and
death. Then I wouldn't be so scared about either of them. But here I am
wondering what's real and what's not. It's difficult to distinguish from time to
time reality from something made up. I don't know exactly what's going on in
this life. It's life sure, but if I can't understand it...and it's my own life I
don't understand...what's the point of it all?
