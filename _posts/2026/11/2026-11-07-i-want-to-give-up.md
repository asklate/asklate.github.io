I want to give up.

There are so many things in this life to give up on. I don't kow why life is
this way. So many thought processes to figure out and yet that's all there is.
No I'm not making sense here. I rarely make sense.

Everything won't make sense. It's life after all. Nothing ever matters anymore.
And why should it? Nope I don't understand it. Make it all go away. It won't
last.

Make me believe in something again. I'm dying inside. There needs to be a way to
overcome all of this. I hate feeling this way.

I'm depressed lately. It doesn't matter though, why should it? Just another day
is all it is. So why bother with this life. I mean don't get me wrong, I
understand the importance of life. Just not *my* life. Why should I? It doesn't
make much sense to me so why bother with any of it all? That's what I want to
know.

So let it be whatever it will be. Don't allow things to get in the way of
thought.
