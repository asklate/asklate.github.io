Life is but an interesting thing at times. We don't always know what this life
will have in store for us. It will all be okay, we tend to tell ourselves. There's
nothing to worry about, is another thing we like to tell ourselves at times. With
all the things going on in life, we cannot know for certain how or when something
might happen or take place. It is but a mystery to me. Life will simply be what
it will be and if that's the case I cannot imagine it to be anything else.

If there's anything this life has taught me it's this. We don't always have the
answers to the questions that we have. That is quite a problem if you ask me. We
cannot always understand everything that goes on in this life. We might want to
have an understanding of it all, but that isn't realistic now, is it? No, I didn't
think so. But who said life is realistic to begin with? Exactly. I'd like to think
life is a realistic place to be, but if it's not? Then it's not, and we don't have
a better place to categorize it all.

My mind doesn't have a way to correctly categorize and map anything. It feels like
it's all over the place at times. I wish I could understand how my mind works,
but I don't have a clue most of the time. It's not a trivial or simple thing to
try and figure out.

I do know one thing for certain, I cannot be focused ont he past. I must look to
the future and live. That's all there is to it. The present is here for a reason,
the future as well. The past can teach me to learn from my mistakes, but other than
that, the past will be there no matter what. I can look back at it, but not linger
too long, or I will be doomed to repeat it. You know the saying, something about
if you don't learn from  your past mistakes you are doomed to repeat them? Yeah!
That one. It's a good quote, or thought...or whatever it is. It's something and
I will accept it for what it is and not go beyond that line of thinking.

There are so many things to worry about in this life. So many things that cannot
be explained away, and so many things that don't want to be believed. It's quite
a thought process that I cannot comprehend at times. I don't know how anything
comes about most days and that's all there is to it.
