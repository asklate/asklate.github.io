So we live in a life that doesn't always make sense. It's a complicated mess at
times and if we cannot figure out what this life is all about, then we are
simply lost. Everything will end up being what it will be. I cannot change the
fact of the matter, I cannot change the matter of the fact. It's simply a
mistaken identity at times. So allow it to be that way. Don't look forward to
that which you cannot look forward to. Allow it to be and you will be okay. Such
a thing in life will continue to be whatever it is meant to be, and we will
never understand what's going on most of the time.

If I had the ability, I think I would want life to make some kind of sense. But
I know that won't be possible. Not all at once mind you. There has to be some
kind of decision in order to be made. I don't know how this all will work out. I
doubt anyone will know how it will be made to work out. Life will be made
manifest eventually. Who knows how that will work out mind you, it's a
complicated mess at times. I don't claim to have all the answers to life. Nor
should I. No, there isn't anything I can do about it all. It's a terrible
thought process that doesn't matter to anyone but me. If it matters to me, is it
real enough? I do not know.

Life will eventually make sense to someone. I am not that person. Who is to know
how this life will end up? I wish I could tell you everything that happens in
life will have a story to go with it, but I can't. It won't always make sense
and that's the terrible truth of it all. Talk about an annoying thought process
that we cannot control at times. It would be nice to be able to understand
whatever there is to understand and don't allow it to become whatever else there
is to think through. Don't let it get in the way, there won't be an answer to
any of it.
