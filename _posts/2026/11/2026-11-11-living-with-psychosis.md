Voices come and go. Hallucinations you might call them. They become annoying.
Imagine voices coming and going when you least expect them. They constantly talk
to you never letting up, never letting go. Talk about a lifetime within another
lifetime. Whatever the case, the voices don't want to stop no matter how much I
want them to.

The voices aren't always nice mind you. Sometimes they are down right evil and
mean. I don't know how else to describe it. They tend to simply be wherever they
want, whenever they want. I can't find a way to stop them. I don't know why that
is. It's an annoying thought process to come across and everything will go about
as it always does. Voices and all.

People don't understand what psychosis can do to you. Personally I want to sit 
down and cry at times. But crying won't do a damn thing. It will only lead to
something more depressed.

Depression isn't a fun thing to have happened in your life. Some people experience
seasonal or situational depression. I on the other hand experience just depression
in general. Major Depressive Disorder is what I have, add Psychotic Features onto
it, and you'll see the kind of trouble that comes my way day in and day out.

Trust me if I could make it all stop, I would. But I don't know how to make it 
stop. There are things in this life that I simply cannot control, and that's not
always a good thing. Sure there are things that no one can control, but that's
expected.

Life is so complex at times, there isn't anything that can e done about it. We
all live in a world full of complexities that don't ever go away. Yeah, there
are times when life isn't fair. Full of surprises, and we don't always get the
chance to see what's out there. Talk about an annoying thought process. But
it's how we deal with life. A simple gesture as it were. Life will be here
forever and a day, it won't end with a simple supernova. We can't be that lucky.
