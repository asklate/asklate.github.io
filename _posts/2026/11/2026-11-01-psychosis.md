Having Major Depressive Disorder with Psychotic Features is difficult at times.
I won't lie. It can be down right, well depressing. Interesting how that all
works out at times, don't you think? Yeah, I think so too.

Can we get an actual explanation of psychosis here? That might shed some light
on the whole story:

> Psychosis is a collection of symptoms characterized by a loss of contact with
> reality, where an individual has difficulty distinguishing between what is real
> and what is not. It is not a standalone diagnosis but rather a symptom
> associated with various mental health conditions, medical illnesses, trauma, or
> substance use. 

Thanks, AI, you're useful for something after all! So, losing grip with reality
as it were. Yeah, I can see that becoming part of my life. I don't enjoy it, 
that much is certain. But, well it's part of life so what am I supposed to do
about it? I don't know. There are many things in this life that don't make sense,
life itself is one of those things.

I'd like for once to be able to say yes this is real or yes this is false. I
can't seem to distinguish such things at times. That's where the problem lies and
where everything else begins.

I firmly believe we are in a simulation of sorts. We're all different parts of
one conscious being. Different parts of their subconscious, or their personality.
A person can have multiple personalities, if we are all a different personality,
then that person must have a lot of personalities that are close together. They
don't always end up being *that* different from each other.
