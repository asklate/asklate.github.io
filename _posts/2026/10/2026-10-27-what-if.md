What if life didn't exist like this? What if we are all creations of a different
thought process and thinking. A lot can happen with what if. What if is just that,
a place to begin, a place to be...a place to think. What if we aren't real. We
could be someone's imagination. Things that take place and not take place, what
will ever happen with those things?
