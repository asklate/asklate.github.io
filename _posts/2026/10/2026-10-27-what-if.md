What if life didn't exist like this? What if we are all creations of a different
thought process and thinking. A lot can happen with what if. What if is just that,
a place to begin, a place to be...a place to think. What if we aren't real. We
could be someone's imagination. Things that take place and not take place, what
will ever happen with those things?

I highly doubt anyone will ever think this is real. There can be several thoughts
on this topic, let's say it's something unique to me though. I know many people
believe in some kind of alternate universe, one were we exist in some kind of
Matrix type reality. Well I'm not talking about the Matrix here. I don't believe
we're all plugged into some giant machine in the far future. I believe we are
different cohesive parts of one person's mind. We are different actors upon a 
stage as it were. All different personalities of one person's mind.

How's that for What If?
