People have a belief system, well not everyone does but a lot of people do.
That's how it all seems to be at times. I wish I had an understanding of how
it's all meant to work out at times. But I don't, and that's simply how this
life turns out to be from time to time. But I know that people believe in a
higher power. A supreme being in charge or some other sort of Godlike creature.
Some might even call Him alien, since He exists in a different plane if
existence. Heaven or some other Celestial Sphere. Whatever you want to call it,
that's what it is. I haven't come up with these thoughts by myself, they have
been around for ages, I am only confirming what is out there as a belief.

Now other people don't believe in a God. They don't believe in Good vs Evil.
That's okay though. There's nothing wrong with that at all. People can believe
in what they want to believe. There are no rules governing for or against it
all. That's simply how this life works at times.

I personally do not know if a God exists or not. Remember I believe that we are
all part of some kind of experiment? Yeah, that. We are different manifestations
of the same person. If that person is God? Then so be it. Whatever the case, if
God wants to make Himself known to mankind, He will. If he wants to remain hidden
that's okay too. We don't have a say in whether He will show Himself to us. People
say they see God in everything Earth has to offer. Does that include the evil things
that happen on this Earth? I highly doubt it. If God is a nice being, a...what's
the word for it? Merciful being? Yeah, that'll work. Then we have nothing to
worry about come what some have determined to be a judgment day. If there is such
a thing. Heck, we might just die and go to live on a cloud. I could get behind
that.

Life doesn't have to mean that everything is unknown to us though. Sure there are
a lot of things that are unknown. That's simply how this life works out. Life can
be whatever it needs to be and there isn't anything we can do about it all. If
life is meant to be something different, then it can be that as well. Not everyone
understands what's meant to be or not be in this life.

Sure people *claim* to have an understanding of what goes on in life. But it
doesn't have to play out that way. I am no different in my thoughts, there isn't
a simple way to overcome or understand whatever there is in life. I will simply
be whatever I am in order to get through this life without too many scratches
on me. If that's how life is meant to be? So be it.

Life will become whatever it's meant to be. I don't have a say in how it's
all turned out to be, unfortunately. I wish I could understand it all, have it
all brought to knowledge in my life. But I doubt there will be a single way to
understand it all at once. That's life in a nutshell I fear. Not everything
will be made to be understood. If that's the case? Then what are we supposed
to do about it all? I simply do not know.

Allow this life to be whatever it needs to be. Don't overthink things. If they
are meant to be overthought? Then they will be. For now, simply embrace all there
is to embrace.

Whatever being is out there in control of the simulation, I sure hope they 
understand how hard I try to make this life a good one. If they don't? Then maybe
I don't want to be with them when I die. I don't have them in my life right now,
what's the point of going back to something I don't remember or recall? Yes, there
are religions that believe our memories were wiped before coming to Earth when
we are born. We don't remember this Godlike figure. We are meant to believe He
exists, and that's all there is to it.

Eh, whatever the case, life will continue to be what it has always been. We 
simply have to try and understand it to some degree by ourselves. Not everyone
has all the answers, that much is certain. I fear that if someone *did* have all
answers to every question imaginable...life wouldn't be the same. It would be
something quite different. So let it be. Let the mystery stick around without a
possible second thought to it all. Don't second guess yourself, don't second
guess anyone.

Trust your gut.
