There is something quietly hopeful about a blank page. It asks for nothing
elaborate. Just a first sentence, a small observation, a reason to return.

The internet is very good at making everything feel immediate. Every thought can
become a post; every moment can be measured by whether someone else noticed it.
I wanted a place with a different rhythm — somewhere that could hold a longer
thought without asking it to perform.

So this is the beginning.

## Pay attention to the small things

A good notebook doesn't need a grand subject. It can begin with a conversation
you keep replaying, a sentence in a book, a strange bit of light on the kitchen
wall, or a question that refuses to go away.

The small things are not always small. Sometimes they are simply the things we
have learned to overlook.

## A practice, not a promise

I don't expect every entry to be polished or profound. Some will be fragments.
Some will change direction halfway through. Others may take a while to find
their shape.

The point is to keep looking, keep asking, and keep a record of what turns up.

For now, that feels like enough.
