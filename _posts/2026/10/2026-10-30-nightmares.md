There are times when nightmares tend to happen. I don't have the answer to 
it all mind you, it's a complicated thought process. But if I can't figure it out,
who can? Yeah, that's what I was thinking. So many thought processes go on through
the mind of us all, and we don't have the answers to any of it. So many things
in this life simply don't make sense. Nightmares are one of those things.

We can dream all we want, but those darn nightmares get in the way in  a way
that never fully make sense. The worst of the worst nightmares come at night
when you least expect them. The dreams tend to stick with you long after you wake
up. There's nothing you cna do about them. Simply acknowledge they are here, and
don't allow them to take over your waking moments. Nightmares are good at that,
they can find a way to destroy your inner thought patterns. That's all there is
to it.

I, for one, would like if nightmares didn't take place. But I can't make that
happen. There are so many thought processes in this life that we simply cannot
make happen because we wish it. We can't avoid nightmares, they will come when
they want to, and we can't easily ignore them. That's life for you.

An unfortunate life mmind you, but life all the same.
