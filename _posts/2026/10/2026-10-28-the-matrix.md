Ah, The Matrix. Let's talk about it for a moment. It's a world enslaved by 
machines in some kind of construct. People are bred in captivity. They are thrown
into the year 1999 when in reality it's far into the future. They think they are
experiencing life as it were, but in reality they are not. They are used as energy
for the machines to thrive off of.

In concept, it's rather spooky. A horror movie of sorts? I think so.

The DejaVu part of it doesn't quite make sense to me. In DejaVu you think you're
experiencing the same thing over again. In The Matrix, the code is being rewritten
so you actually see the same thing twice. It's not the same thing, but we can let
it slide for now.

Being freed from The Matrix, I'm not sure is actually a good thing. Sure you get
to experience what *real* life is like, but would you really want that? If you 
could go through life not knowing the truth...would you prefer that over knowing
the actual reality of life? I think I would prefer living in a fantasy world
rather than knowing and trying to survive the harsh truth of reality.

Then again, knowing that you can hack into The Matrix at any given time and do
basically whatever you want to do? That's something unique. I mean who wouldn't
want to fly like Neo? Superman is cool, I get it. I imagine after a while it would
become trivial though and would lose its *coolness*. (Is that a word? I'm not
certain it is!)

Whatever the case, if you're in The Matrix might as well enjoy life while you can.
The reality that is, might not be what you expected.
